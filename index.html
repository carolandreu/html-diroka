DIROKA TATTOO STUDIO

Welcome to Diroka Tattoo Studio, proudly serving the Denver metro area from our studio in Aurora, Colorado, where art and elegance merge to create an unforgettable experience. At Diroka, we stand apart from other tattoo and piercing studios with our modern, upscale atmosphere — completely free from negativity and bad vibes.

We are committed to the highest standards of hygiene and safety, ensuring every client feels secure and comfortable. To complete the VIP experience, we also provide personalized attention, complimentary drinks, and snacks that make every visit special.

OUR TEAM

DIEGO JUAREZ

Tattoo Artist

Geometric • Dotwork • Black & Grey

As the founder of Diroka Tattoo Studio and with over 15 years of experience, Diego Juarez has built a reputation for creating large-scale custom tattoos that flow naturally with the body. Internationally recognized for his award-winning geometric work, he combines precision, creativity, and artistic vision to transform every idea into a timeless piece of art. From intricate geometric compositions to black & grey realism and dotwork, every tattoo is designed exclusively for the client and executed with uncompromising attention to detail.

ARNOLD CAMARGO

Tattoo Artist

Color Realism • Portraits • Anime Tattoos

For over 10 years, Arnold has been turning ideas into unforgettable works of art through tattooing. Known for his vibrant color realism, lifelike portraits, and bold anime pieces, he approaches every project with precision, creativity, and a commitment to excellence. Constantly refining his craft, Arnold believes every tattoo should tell a story and become a piece his clients are proud to wear for a lifetime.
