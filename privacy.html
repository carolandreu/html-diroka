LEGAL & PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Effective September 13, 2026

Diroka Tattoo Studio respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through our website, why we use it, the service providers that help us operate the site, and the choices available to you.

1. Information you provide

When you submit a booking request, you may provide your name, email address, phone number, preferred artist, requested service, preferred date, tattoo or piercing details, body placement, approximate size, style or color preferences, a reference-image link, and any other information included in your message.

Please do not include sensitive personal information that is not needed for us to understand and respond to your request.

2. Information collected automatically

With your permission, we use Google Analytics and Meta Pixel to understand visits, page interactions, booking-button clicks, advertising referrals, and successful booking requests. These services may process device and browser information, cookie identifiers, general location derived from an IP address, page URLs, and interaction data.

If you decline optional tracking, Meta Pixel is not loaded and Google Analytics cookie storage remains disabled. Google Consent Mode may still send limited, cookieless consent and page signals to Google.

3. How we use information

  • To respond to questions and booking requests.
  • To discuss, schedule, and provide tattoo or piercing services.
  • To operate, protect, and improve our website and customer experience.
  • To measure advertising performance and understand how visitors find us.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations and prevent misuse or fraud.

4. Service providers

We use trusted providers to operate this website:

  • Formspree processes and delivers booking-form submissions. Formspree Privacy Policy.
  • Google Analytics provides website measurement when permitted. Google Privacy Policy.
  • Meta Pixel measures advertising results when permitted. Meta Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub Pages hosts and delivers the website.

These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not sell personal information.

5. Cookies and your choices

On your first visit, you can accept or decline optional analytics and advertising tracking. You can change your selection at any time by choosing Cookie Preferences at the bottom of the website. You may also restrict cookies through your browser settings.

6. Data retention and security

We keep booking inquiries only for as long as reasonably needed to respond, provide services, maintain business records, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no method of internet transmission or electronic storage is completely secure.

7. Your privacy requests

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information, or to object to certain uses. To make a request, contact us using the information below. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

8. Children

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through the website.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our website, services, or privacy practices change. The effective date above will show when the latest version took effect.
