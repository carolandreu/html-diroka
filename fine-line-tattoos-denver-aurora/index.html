A More Personal Approach

Fine line work that fits the idea — and the person wearing it.

Fine line tattooing is about restraint, proportion, and intentional detail. At Diroka, the goal is not to make every tattoo look the same. We work from your idea, references, placement, and preferred level of detail to create a piece that feels natural on the body.

For small tattoos, you can bring a reference and we can reproduce or adapt the concept to the size and placement you want. For larger or more custom projects, the design is developed individually around your vision and anatomy.

Fine Line Tattoo FAQ

Before you book.

Can I bring a tattoo reference?+

Yes. For small tattoos, you can bring a reference and we can reproduce or adapt the concept to your preferred size and placement. Larger custom projects are developed individually around your references, ideas, and anatomy.

Do you offer consultations for fine line tattoos?+

Yes. Tattoo consultations are free. They are especially useful for larger, custom, or placement-sensitive projects where we want to discuss size, detail, and artist fit before booking.

Are fine line tattoos only black and grey?+

No. Fine line work can be black and grey, but it can also include selective color, soft shading, or small color accents depending on the design and artist.

How much does a fine line tattoo cost?+

Pricing depends on size, placement, detail, and the artist. Send us your idea, reference, approximate size, and placement through the booking form and the shop can guide you from there.

Do you take clients from Denver?+

Absolutely. Diroka is physically located in Aurora and regularly serves clients from Denver and surrounding metro areas.
